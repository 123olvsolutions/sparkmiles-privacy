Privacy Policy for SparkMiles

Effective Date: November 2026

SparkMiles ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our mobile application, SparkMiles.

1. Background Location Data Collection

SparkMiles collects precise location data to enable automated driving mileage and route tracking for tax deduction logging, EVEN WHEN THE APP IS CLOSED OR NOT IN USE.

Because Spark delivery drivers need to navigate, take calls, or switch between apps while driving, background location access is strictly required to calculate trip distances and generate accurate IRS-compliant mileage logs without requiring the screen to stay active continuously.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculating driving miles and tracking trip routes in the background.
  • Generating downloadable mileage logs for tax deduction purposes.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or precise geolocation data with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising, marketing, or tracking purposes.

4. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA Disclosures)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights regarding your personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to request details regarding the categories of personal information and background location data we collect.
  • Right to Delete: You can request that we delete your stored mileage logs and location history.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: Under CPRA, precise geolocation data is categorized as Sensitive Personal Information. You have the right to limit our use of precise location data strictly to core mileage-tracking functionality.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: SparkMiles does NOT sell or share any user personal information or precise location data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Location data is retained only as long as necessary to provide tax mileage reports to you. You can request account or data deletion at any time by contacting us at the email address below.
